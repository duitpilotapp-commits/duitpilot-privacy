Privacy Policy for DuitPilot

Effective Date: June 30, 2026

1. What This Is About

Hi. This privacy policy explains how DuitPilot handles your financial information. The core idea is simple: your data belongs to you. We built DuitPilot to pull information from your banking notifications on your phone, not our servers. This policy lays out exactly what we read, what we keep, and what we never touch.

2. How We Read Your Notifications

DuitPilot needs permission to read your notifications so it can automatically track your spending. Here's how it actually works.

The reading happens in memory. When a notification comes in from a bank or wallet app, DuitPilot reads it while it's still sitting in your phone's temporary memory. From that notification text, we extract three pieces of information: the amount you spent, the merchant name, and a category hint.

The raw text is thrown away immediately. The original notification contains sensitive stuff like names, account numbers, and reference codes. We discard all of that the moment we finish extracting the three pieces we need. It never gets saved to your phone's storage and it never leaves your device.

We only read from banks and wallets. DuitPilot ignores notifications from anything that isn't a financial app. Your messages, social media, email, games, and every other app on your phone? We skip them completely and never read them at all.

The apps DuitPilot currently reads from are: Maybank, MAE, CIMB, RHB, Public Bank, Touch 'n Go eWallet, GrabPay, Boost, ShopeePay, Google Wallet, and DuitNow transfers that show up in your banking apps.

3. Where Your Data Lives

On your device. Your expense ledger is stored locally on your phone. This includes transaction amounts, dates, merchant names, and categories. Your category corrections, budgets, and spending patterns all stay right there on your device.

In the cloud (optionally). If you want your data to survive a phone reinstall or upgrade, DuitPilot can sync your structured transactions to a cloud database. Only the basics get synced: amount, date, merchant, and category. No personal names, no raw notification text, nothing sensitive. Your cloud account is completely anonymous (we never ask for your name or email to create one). Every transaction you sync is locked to your account only, using a security model that makes sure no one else and no unauthorized party can see your data. Raw notification text is never, ever part of this sync.

We also download updates to our parsing rules from the cloud, like new merchant keywords. These updates come one way only, from us to your phone, and they carry zero personal information about you.

4. How Data Moves Between Devices and Cloud

When your data travels between your phone and our cloud service, it's encrypted using HTTPS. This means nobody can eavesdrop on or tamper with your information while it's in transit.

5. We Don't Sell, Trade, or Share Your Data

Your personal and financial data is yours. We don't sell it, rent it, trade it, or share it with third parties. We use your data for one reason only: to run the app and show you your ledger and budgets. We don't build advertising profiles from your spending. We don't hand your transaction history to outside companies. We don't do analytics on your data.

6. You Stay in Control

Your data is always yours to manage. You can delete your transaction history directly from the app, or you can clear all of the app's data through your phone settings. When you delete your data on the device, the matching records in the cloud also get deleted.

7. Kids and DuitPilot

DuitPilot is meant for adults who are managing their own money. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13.

8. If We Change This Policy

We might update this privacy policy as DuitPilot evolves. When we make a change that matters, we'll update the date at the top of this page. We encourage you to check back here once in a while.
